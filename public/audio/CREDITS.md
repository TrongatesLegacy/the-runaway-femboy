# Audio credits

Every sound here comes from a **CC0 / public-domain** source, or was generated for this project. Attribution isn't legally required, but the authors are credited below as thanks. No CC-BY, NC or ND material is used.

License link for every entry: CC0 1.0 Universal, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Processing applied to everything: converted to 44.1 kHz, loudness-normalized (music about -16 LUFS, ambient about -26 LUFS, SFX peak-limited to -1 dBTP with roughly matched loudness), leading silence trimmed on SFX, and encoded to Ogg Vorbis (q5) and MP3 (160 kbps for music and ambient, 128 kbps for SFX). Composites and pitch changes are noted per file.
